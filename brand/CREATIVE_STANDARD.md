# NovaMatter Creative Standard — New Matter. New Meaning.

**Owner:** NovaMatter creative team  
**Updated:** 2026-11-19  
**Applies to:** HeyGen productions, approved YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok videos, website video, product launch assets and related marketing materials.

## Creative purpose

NovaMatter bridges technological intelligence with thoughtful human well-being and environmental responsibility. Every campaign should be coherent under one parent brand while demonstrating a *real* value proposition of NovaLife or NovaLens. Premium, cinematic and energetic does not mean vague or dramatic. The aesthetic must be supported by real product footage, accurate claims and a meaningful call to action.

The brand sign-off is **"New Matter. New Meaning."** Use it as a signature when it serves the story, not a constant interruption. Do not duplicate the same video template across dozens of posts.

### What belongs in an effective short
1. **0–2 seconds: hook.** Establish a real frustration, consequential question or observable action without wasting time on an animated logo intro.
2. **2–6 seconds: product context.** Show what problem is being solved and name the product quickly.
3. **6–20 seconds: verified demonstration.** Show current real UI or a truthful, explicitly illustrative mechanism; make the benefit comprehensible.
4. **20–28 seconds: differentiation.** Explain one specific reason that feature matters, with measured language and supporting visual evidence.
5. **Final seconds: one conversion action.** Use the approved logo/wordmark, a tested App Store or product URL, and one invitation. No dead silence or empty filler.

Shorter videos should compress these beats intelligently, not rush through four claims at once. Format can vary: documentary, clean cinematic, kinetic editorial graphics, genuine screen recordings, tasteful founder segments, or visual scientific explainers.

## Art direction

- **Personality:** contemporary engineering precision meets natural systems; confident, humane, curious, clean and memorable.
- **Palette:** use verified company colors from approved brand references; avoid default HeyGen colors unless the approved design actually uses them. Use restrained contrast, purposeful accent highlights and controlled light.
- **Type:** one clean contemporary type family (or approved matched pair), large enough for phone screens, with minimal simultaneous reading tasks.
- **Layout:** one focal point per beat; respect platform-safe margins and subtitles.
- **Motion:** cuts and transformations must clarify meaning. Avoid repeating decorative grids, white slides, stock AI particles, glitch noise or unnecessary logo twirls.
- **Footage:** real user journeys and actual product interfaces dominate. Make every AI-generated background clearly supportive; never fabricate an app screen, user review, or product result.
- **Sound:** licensed original/equivalent modern electronic music with emotional progression; clear intelligible voiceover at normal cadence, no clipping or competing dialogue.
- **Subtitles:** accurate, synchronized, accessible and rendered in-platform-safe regions; evaluate with sound muted.
- **Output:** vertical 9:16 for Shorts/Reels/TikTok; prefer 1080×1920. Verify the actual file with metadata. When the account limits native render to 720p, disclose the limit, consider approved upscaling with visual QA, and do not describe it as genuine native 1080p.

## Product truth and claims

### NovaLife
- Focus on authentic currently available Today, Track, Plan, reflection/journaling and other verified experience flows.
- Frame it as an evidence-informed personal well-being and life-planning aid, **not** therapy, diagnosis, treatment, clinical advice or guaranteed recovery.
- Confirm what the public App Store build actually includes before advertising an advanced source-only feature.
- Never expose journal entries, health data or private personal information in screen captures or tracking.

### NovaLens
- Lead with verified product identification, environmental evidence, confidence, source freshness and clear comparison flows.
- Withhold definitive scores when required evidence is missing; do not imply absent emissions data equals zero.
- Clearly separate CO₂ and CO₂e. Label units, source boundaries, data confidence and examples.
- Avoid blanket product or brand sustainability guarantees and unsupported procurement/shipping claims.

## Eight-dimension publishing scorecard

Rate each 0–5 points (maximum 40). **Pass threshold: 36/40**, with no critical failure.

| Dimension | Evidence | Critical |
|---|---|---|
| Opening hook and audience fit | Hook works on the first phone-screen viewing | No |
| Demonstrated, current product benefit | Real product behavior clearly shown | Yes |
| Brand identity and correct logos | Approved parent/product marks and colors | Yes |
| Cinematography, motion and legibility | Intentional visual hierarchy and sharp output | No |
| Voice, music, sound rights | Clear, well-mixed licensed audio | No |
| Captions and accessibility | Readable at mobile size and accurate | No |
| Claims, privacy, health and environment | Checked against authentic evidence | Yes |
| CTA and destination | One working, accurate link to a verified destination | Yes |

### Mandatory preflight
- [ ] Verify actual source logo files, parent/product relationship, version and proof screens.
- [ ] Verify real output resolution, orientation, legibility and audio.
- [ ] Watch whole cut on a phone, first with sound and then muted.
- [ ] Verify the first two seconds communicate a reason to stay.
- [ ] Verify one useful on-screen product action, not an attractive but empty montage.
- [ ] Verify captions, music license, contrast, spelling, pronunciation and correct icon.
- [ ] Verify no unapproved treatment or sustainability claim.
- [ ] Verify the final frame and working CTA; no blank tail.
- [ ] Record score and approval by a human reviewer.
- [ ] If any critical item fails, **do not publish or use credits to repeat the same uncorrected setup**.

## Production workflow

1. Review last five videos, paid credits and performance signals. Identify an original concept.
2. Approve a script and storyboard matched to actual app assets.
3. Run an optional second creative review using sanitized copy; never share private code, credentials or user data externally without explicit authorization.
4. Assemble real screenshots, product video, official logo artwork, music, voice and subtitle source.
5. Produce within approved account limits; do not generate without the required credit budget.
6. Inspect the entire exported MP4 and score it. Reject weak or unbranded drafts.
7. Package platform-native titles, descriptions, covers, subtitles, legal rights, and links.
8. **The founder approves and publishes** through authorized social tools. Do not publish from an automated creation run.
9. Measure retention, meaningful clicks, installs or leads; record one lesson for the next iteration.

## Brand continuity and growth

Keep **one flagship NovaMatter YouTube channel**, with recognizable NovaLife and NovaLens series. Alternate high-energy discovery films with educational evidence-led content, actual UI demonstrations and occasional founder-led narratives. Build trusted owned destinations: functioning product pages, app listings, optional email, documented support/privacy and accurately labeled feature information.

Great reach that does not help a real person understand or use either product is not the objective. The outcome is earned trust, product value, sustainable adoption and, where appropriate, qualified commercial interest.
